Privacy

No trackers, no cookies, no analytics. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

This website

No tracking here

This site has no trackers, cookies or analytics, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. Like any web host, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses. We don't use them to track you.

The launcher

Nothing comes to us

The CleanYourRoku launcher collects no data and sends nothing to us. It has no accounts, ads or analytics. It opens the apps you chose and plays the media you linked.

Jellyfin

Your media stays yours

Jellyfin runs on your own network, so your videos and music stream from your server to your Roku. Your server address and API key stay in your own build, and none of it comes to us.

Roku and your apps

They have their own policies

The launcher doesn't change what Roku or the apps you open collect. Their own privacy policies apply. The privacy settings guide shows how to turn off much of Roku's tracking.

Optional ad muter

One tool sends text out

The optional ad muter is separate from the launcher. If you run it, text transcribed from your room's audio goes to Anthropic using your own API key. Anthropic's terms apply to that text.

Contacting us

Only what you send

If you email support@cleanyourroku.com, we see what you send and use it to reply. GitHub issues and pull requests are public, and GitHub's privacy policy applies there.
