Recommender systems decide what you see first. CleanYourRoku puts that choice back with you, starting with your Roku's home screen.

Privacy settings

Ten minutes with your remote. Hide the recommendation rows, turn off personalized ads and reset your advertising ID. You can undo all of it.

Step 1 · About 20 minutes

Install the launcher

Turn on Roku's developer mode, pick your apps, pull their logos, then zip it and use Delete, Upload, Install. The current build lists 20 apps, and you choose your own.

Network

Why do I get a 403 error?

Your Roku is limiting network control. Set Network access to Permissive under Control by mobile apps. Any device on your Wi-Fi can then send commands, so that's your call.

Publishing a beta

Is the LaunchApp warning a problem?

No, not for a beta. A launcher opens other apps, so the scan flags it. A warning isn't an error, and the beta still publishes and runs.

Publishing a beta

What if my package is over 4 MB?

That's a warning, not a blocker, but smaller loads faster. Shrink big images, remove ones you don't use and use JPEG for photos.

Everyday use

Why does Back go to Roku's home?

That's Roku, not a bug, and a sideloaded app can't change it. Put CleanYourRoku first in your app list so it's one click away.

Everyday use

Can I have more than one home screen?

Sort of. Roku allows one sideloaded app at a time, but you can keep several zips and install the one you want. It takes about a minute.

Safety

Does it uninstall or block apps?

No. It only changes which apps you see on its screen. Everything else stays installed. It curates, so a kids' profile isn't a parental control.

Safety

Will it break my Roku?

Developer mode is an official Roku feature, and nothing here modifies your Roku's system software. To undo it, delete the app and turn developer mode off.

Compatibility

Does it work on my Roku model?

It's been tested on one Roku streaming player. Others should work, but that isn't confirmed. If you try it, please share your notes.

Honest Limits

A tool about agency should be honest about what it can and can't do.

  • Tested on two devices. One Roku streaming player and one Vizio TV. Other models may behave differently.
  • One sideloaded app at a time. Installing a new zip replaces the old one.
  • Back goes to Roku's home. An app can't override that.
  • The ad panel on Roku's home stays. No setting removes it. The launcher is an alternative home, not a change to Roku's.
  • It curates, it doesn't control. It doesn't uninstall, block or lock anything. It's not a parental control tool.
  • My Album's sample content is made up. Its sample videos and music need your own Jellyfin server, or you can replace them.
