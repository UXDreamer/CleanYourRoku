# Contributing

Thanks for being here. You don't need to be a developer to help. Some of the most useful contributions are just notes about your own TV.

## The mission

This project exists to help prove one thing: **control should be the default.** Tracking should be off unless you turn it on, and your own apps should come first. Every Roku model tested, every setting documented and every before-and-after shared makes that case stronger.

---

## Ways to help

### Got a different Roku model? Add your notes.

This project has only been tested on one Roku streaming player and one Vizio TV. Your device is probably different, and that's exactly what's needed.

Tell us:

- Your device, for example "Roku Ultra 2024," "Roku Streaming Stick 4K" or "TCL Roku TV, 55 inch"
- Your Roku OS version (**Settings → System → About**)
- Which steps worked, which didn't, and any menu names that were different

### Share your before-and-after

Did you clean up your Roku? Show us. We'd love:

- **Screenshots or photos**, before and after
- **A sentence or two** on what changed for you. Did it change what you watch, or how often you scroll?

**Before you share a photo,** check what's in the background: names, mail, faces, anything personal. Blur or crop it out. Your privacy is the point of this project.

### Fix or improve something

Typos, unclear steps, a setting that moved in a newer Roku update: all welcome. If you add a step, please include the **why** along with the **how**. That's the rule for every guide here.

---

## How to contribute

**The easy way (no Git needed):** [open an issue](https://github.com/dlpressl/CleanYourRoku/issues/new) and paste your notes. You can drag screenshots straight into the box.

**The Git way:**

1. Fork the repo.
2. Make your change. Put images in `images/` and keep them reasonably sized.
3. Open a pull request with a short description of what you changed and why.

Never include API keys, tokens or passwords. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
