# Credits

## Inspiration

**[Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv)** is the project that started it all. Gathin used Claude Code to clean the ads and clutter off an Android TV, shared it, and it went viral. That showed a lot of people, me included, that you don't have to accept the home screen your TV ships with.

cleanyourtv covers Android and Google TVs. CleanYourRoku takes the same idea to Roku, which works completely differently and needed its own approach. Thank you, Gathin, for opening the door.
