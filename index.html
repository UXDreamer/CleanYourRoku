CleanYourRoku

Debloat your Roku TV over Wi‑Fi and get an Apple TV‑style home.

Turn off the tracking that's on by default, then replace the cluttered home screen with one that shows only your apps, in the order you choose.

The problem

Your apps come last.

Roku's home screen leads with what Roku picked for you. The apps you chose sit below about seven rows of recommendations, beside an ad panel you can't turn off.

~7

recommendation rows above the apps you installed

On

tracking and ad personalization, by default

0

settings that remove the ad panel

How it works

Two steps. Start with no code at all.

Step 0 · No code

Privacy settings

Ten minutes with your remote. Hide the recommendation rows, turn off personalized ads, reset your advertising ID, and switch off content recognition on Roku-built TVs.

Your apps move up. The ad panel stays, because no setting can remove it.

Read the Step 0 guide →

Step 1 · About 20 minutes

The CleanYourRoku launcher

A home screen that shows only your apps, in the order you choose. No recommendation rows, no ad panel.

Installs with Roku's official developer mode. Nothing is hacked, and you can remove it anytime.

Why it matters

Use AI as a partner. Keep the decision.

Recommender systems decide a lot of what we watch, buy and see, and they're built to serve the company first. This project was built with AI, and shaped by the moments I disagreed with it. When the AI said publishing a Roku beta wasn't possible, I tried anyway, and it worked.

  1. 01

    Know

    Understand what the system is doing, and why.

  2. 02

    Question

    Defaults aren't neutral. Ask who they serve.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Keep the final choice for yourself.

Control should be the default.

Most people can't, and shouldn't have to, build their own home screen. TVs should offer a clear yes or no at setup, keep tracking off unless you turn it on, and include a "my apps only" mode.
